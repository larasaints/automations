# 🤖 Hands-Free Google Forms & Gemini AI Real Estate Assistant

A smart, fully automated system built on Make.com that handles incoming real estate leads completely on its own. It watches a Google Sheet for new responses, sorts leads by property choice, adds viewing appointments to Google Calendar, and uses **Gemini AI** to send instant, personalized email replies via Gmail. It runs automatically in the background every 15 minutes, 24/7.

## 🚀 The Core Business Challenge
When customers ask about a rental or condo, they want answers fast. If you take too long to reply, they look somewhere else. This system acts as a 24/7 digital assistant for **Casa De Lara by SMDC** properties (Shore Residences Pasay & Jazz Residences Makati). It guarantees that every single inquirer gets a custom pricing breakdown and property overview in their inbox in under 15 minutes—completely hands-free, without you needing to do any manual work.

## 🛠️ How It Works (Step-by-Step Pipeline)
The system wakes up automatically every 15 minutes and processes new leads using these simple steps:

1. **The Inbound Gate (`Google Sheets`)**: The system checks your main spreadsheet (`Google Form Leads - CDL`) for any new rows submitted by users from your public Google Form.
2. **The Sorting Hat (`Router`)**: It reads what the customer selected and instantly splits them into the right property track:
   * **Path A:** Customers looking for **Jazz Residences (Makati City)**.
   * **Path B:** Customers looking for **Shore Residences (Pasay City)**.
3. **The Scheduler (`Google Calendar`)**: It automatically adds a tracking marker or booking slot to your Google Calendar so you never lose track of an appointment or client timeline.
4. **The Brains (`Gemini AI`)**: The system passes the customer's name, budget, and details to Gemini AI. The AI automatically calculates the totals and drafts a friendly, professional email pitch.
5. **The Delivery (`Gmail`)**: The system copies the AI's custom text response and instantly sends a clean outbound email straight to the customer's inbox.
6. **The Safety Guard (`Retry`)**: If an internet glitch or API speed limit causes the system to miss a step, the workflow automatically catches the error and retries the action, making sure no hot lead ever drops.
